If your question is about a purchase, please include your Support ID: open Focused, go to the You tab and tap the ID under your name to copy it. (You → Contact us opens an email with it already filled in.)

Quick answers

  • Get my purchases back: open any Pro screen (You → Get Pro or Pro+) and tap Restore at the top right. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.
  • New phone or reinstall: Focused backs itself up to your own iCloud (iPhone) or Google account (Android). After reinstalling, choose Restore on the “Welcome back” screen. You can see the last backup in You → Backup.
  • Bedtime Mode doesn’t block: iPhone — allow Screen Time access when Focused asks (Settings → Screen Time → Apps with Screen Time Access). Android — allow Usage access and Display over other apps for Focused.
  • No reminders: turn on notifications for Focused in your phone’s Settings, and check You → Reminders and alerts.
  • How things work: You → Tips and You → Questions and answers explain every feature; You → Tips → Replay the app tour shows the walkthrough again.

Your data

Focused has no accounts. Your tasks and sessions stay on your phone and in your own backup. Read the Privacy Policy or ask us to delete your data.
