Delete your data

Focused: Task Timer & Fitness · developed by day2day.app · Last updated: November 23, 2026

Focused has no accounts. Your habits, moods, sessions and settings live on your phone and in your own private backup, not with us. The only data held on our side is a random Support ID and your purchase records, which our purchase service RevenueCat keeps so purchases can be validated and restored.

How to ask us to delete it

  1. Open Focused and go to the Profile tab. Tap your Support ID at the top to copy it.
  2. Email zunayed.apps@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data – Focused” and paste your Support ID. (Profile → Contact us opens an email with the Support ID already filled in.)
  3. We delete the data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

If you no longer have the app, email us anyway and tell us roughly when and on which store you bought — we will find and delete the matching records.

What is deleted

  • Your Support ID and the purchase and receipt records linked to it at RevenueCat.
  • The basic technical details stored with them (app version, platform, store country/currency).
  • Your email to us, once we have confirmed the deletion.

What is kept

  • Google Play and the Apple App Store keep their own records of your payments. We do not control those; they are kept under the stores' own policies.
  • Because your purchases belong to your Google or Apple account, you can still get them back later with Restore Purchases in the app — this creates a new record.

Delete the data on your phone yourself

  • Everything in the app: uninstall Focused. This removes all of its data from your phone right away.
  • Android backup: Focused's backup is part of your Google account's device backup. You can remove it in your phone's Settings → Google → Backup, or in Google Drive → Backups.
  • iPhone backup: if you reinstall Focused and choose Start fresh, your old iCloud copy is replaced the next time Focused backs up. Turning off iCloud for Focused in Settings → [your name] → iCloud stops the backup.
