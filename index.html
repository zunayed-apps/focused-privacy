Privacy Policy

Focused: Task Timer & Fitness · developed by day2day.app · Last updated: November 22, 2026

In short: Focused has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking. What you create stays on your device and in your own private backup (your iCloud on iPhone, your Google backup on Android) — we never receive it. The only information we (through RevenueCat) receive is what is needed to process and restore in-app purchases.

1. Your data: on your device and in your own backup

Your habits, moods, timer and focus sessions, presets, fitness logs, settings, profile name, coins, unlocked animations and music choices are stored on your device. We do not receive or store this data.

So you don't lose them when you reinstall or change phones, they are also backed up to your own cloud storage — never to us:

  • iPhone: Focused automatically saves a copy to your iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage when you leave the app. It is private to your Apple ID; we cannot access it. If you reinstall, Focused asks before restoring it. You can see the last backup in Profile → Backup.
  • Android: if backup is turned on in your device settings, Android's automatic backup saves these files to your personal Google account and restores them when you reinstall.

Your profile photo, any music files you import and your Bedtime app choices are not included in the backup.

2. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. We use RevenueCat to validate and restore purchases. To do this RevenueCat receives:

  • a random Support ID generated on your device (it is not linked to your name or email),
  • purchase and receipt information from the App Store / Google Play, and
  • basic technical information such as app version, platform and store country/currency.

See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

3. Support ID

Your Support ID (shown under your name in Profile) is a random code created on your device. If you contact us about a purchase, sharing it lets us find your purchase records. It is included in your backup, so it stays the same when you reinstall and restore. Without a backup, a reinstall creates a new Support ID; your purchases can still be recovered with "Restore purchases".

4. Bedtime Mode and app blocking

  • iPhone: Bedtime Mode uses Apple's Screen Time framework. The apps you pick are handled by iOS as private tokens — we cannot see which apps you chose, and nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Android: Bedtime Mode uses Usage access (to detect which app is open during your sleep hours), Display over other apps (to show the block screen), See installed apps (so you can choose which apps to block), and a foreground service, battery-optimisation exemption and start-at-boot (so your schedule keeps working). All of this is processed only on your device.

You can revoke any permission in your device settings; Bedtime Mode will stop working without them.

5. Notifications and widgets

Habit reminders and timer alerts are scheduled locally on your device. We do not register your device with a push-notification server. Home-screen widgets read your data from storage on your device.

6. If you email us

If you contact us, we receive your email address and the message you send (for example your Support ID) and use them only to reply to you.

7. Children

Focused is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your rights

We hold no copy of your content: it lives on your device and in your own iCloud or Google backup. Deleting the app removes it from the device; a backup in your own cloud account is replaced by the next backup (for example after you choose "Start fresh"). To ask about or delete the purchase records linked to your Support ID, email us with your Support ID. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA/UK or California) you may have additional rights; contact us to exercise them.

9. Third-party services

  • Apple App Store and Google Play Billing — payment processing
  • RevenueCat — purchase validation and restoring purchases
  • Apple iCloud (iPhone) and Android backup to your Google account — your own private backup of your app data; we cannot access it

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes take effect when published here, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
